The bundled source lists cities in 31 provinces. One city record has no latitude/longitude and is omitted from the selectable city list. The application uses coordinates for prayer-time calculation with its existing PrayTimes Tehran method and parameter adjustments. Verify local prayer-time expectations before public release.
